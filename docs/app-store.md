# The App Store answers

Ready to paste into App Store Connect once he joins the Developer Program.
Every figure here is from the game as it is (2 November); change this page
when the game changes. Counted limits are noted beside each field.

## Privacy

**App Privacy ("nutrition label"): Data Not Collected.** The game makes no
network calls at all: no analytics, no accounts, no ads, no tracking. The
save stays on the phone (the web view's storage and the app's own copy,
which the phone's iCloud backup carries; Apple's backup is not collection
by the developer). Purchases go through Apple, which Apple does not count
as the developer collecting.

**Privacy policy** (a page is required even when nothing is collected; host
it anywhere public, for example a GitHub Pages page of the repo):

> **Mythic Mulligan privacy policy**
>
> Mythic Mulligan does not collect, store or share any personal
> information. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and
> makes no network connections. Your progress is saved only on your own
> device, and in your device's own backup if you use one. Purchases are
> handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. If you
> turn on Reminders, they are scheduled on your device and sent by it; no
> server is involved. Questions: [his email].

## Age rating

Answers for Apple's questionnaire, and the rating they should give: **4+**.

- Violence (cartoon, realistic, graphic): **None**.
- Horror or fear themes: **None**. (A judgement call: the Demonic, Dread,
  Inferno and Void looks are small pixel-art outfits, nothing frightening.
  If he would rather be safe, **Infrequent/Mild** makes it 9+.)
- Sexual content, nudity, mature themes, profanity, drugs, alcohol,
  tobacco: **None**.
- Simulated gambling: **None**. Wagers are short golf rounds for prizes;
  nothing is staked. The bags give random clubs, and their chances are
  shown before buying, which Apple's rules ask for.
- Contests: **None** (no real prizes).
- Unrestricted web access, user-generated content, messaging, location:
  **No**.
- In-app purchases: **Yes**.
- Medical or treatment information: **No**.

## Notes for App Review

> No account or login is needed. Everything sold is in the Pro Shop (the
> cart button): sovereign packs, the Starter Pack and Club Membership on
> the Offers tab, the Tour Pass on the Pass tab. Restore Purchases is on
> the Offers tab. The bags on the Bags tab give random clubs and are bought
> with sovereigns; their chances are behind the ? on that tab. The game
> plays by itself; tap the course or open the menus to see it work.
